 Changelogs 

*   ### Version 2.2.0

    *   - New: support for party size mods (More Pawns and similar), extra hired pawns get their own slots automatically
    *   - New: "Allow hired pawns to level from EXP" (Options + Debug), hired pawns level up from the EXP they earn and the status screen shows their EXP
    *   - New: DLC appraisal roll editor, set physical, magick, knockdown, weight and the bonus on appraised gear, kept inside the game's own roll limits for that item (pawns and Arisen gear), with Best roll and Original roll
    *   - New: "No skill (empty slot)" option to clear a skill slot
    *   - New: "Also use this name as the moniker", the name some menus and the party list show
    *   - New: Reset and Restore core skills buttons
    *   - New: Validate Pawn flags appraisal stats and bonuses a real drop can't roll
    *   - Fixed: Silent, Less and Rare chatter didn't stop pawns talking
    *   - Fixed: hired pawns that drowned, died or were dismissed lost their settings, they now come back when the same pawn is hired again
    *   - Fixed: restoring gear now also puts back the original appraisal roll
    *   - Fixed: a rare error when a pawn appeared a frame before its character finished loading

**The Pawn Controller**  
Preview build 2.2.0  
Formerly Hired Pawn Equipment Override  
  
  
Your new hire turns up in rags, in the wrong vocation, telling you wolves hunt in packs for the fortieth time? Open the menu and fix all of it.  
  
Full control over all your pawns, from their gear, vocation, skills, personality, voice, knowledge, even their face.  
  
  
────────────────────  
  
  
**This is a preview**  
  
A lot has been added since the last version. Most options have been tested but it's impossible to test for every situation, so please be careful when using the mod, make backups and please report any issues you find.  
  
If you find any issues or crashes when applying options, please share the REFramework log (re2_framework_log.txt in your game folder).  
  

> Back up your saves first. Some changes are permanent and get written to your save. The mod records each pawn's original values so you can revert any options, but backing up your save is still highly advised.

  
  
────────────────────  
  
  
**Hired Pawns**  
Settings stick to the pawn, not the slot. Hire someone new and they start with a clean slate, hire the same pawn again (or summon them back after they drown) and their settings come back.  
Using a party size mod like More Pawns? Extra hired pawns get their own slots automatically.  
  
**Equipment**  
Every slot has its own searchable dropdown covering every piece of gear in the game, now updated to pull straight from the game.

Set the enhancement level and Dragonforge any of the pawn's equipment.

Each slot has 'Restore' buttons that shows the original item.

Want a Warrior in a Mage hat? Enable 'Ignore vocation restrictions for equipment overrides' in Options + Debug.

  
  

**New in 2.1**: DLC special skills. Pick up to four for any weapon or armour piece (except mantles).

Keep 'Only show skills that can roll on the item' ticked and the list filters to what CAN roll on a drop.

**New in 2.2**: edit the appraisal roll on DLC gear (physical, magick, knockdown, weight and the bonus), always kept within what a real drop of that item could roll.

Equipment changes show on the pawn immediately, no pause or reload needed.

Note: Hired pawns don't keep gear that gets replaced, 'Restore' restores the original item.  
  
**Vocation + Skills**  
Change their vocation, vocation rank, level, all 6 weapon skills, core skills and augments, including the 7th book slot.

Set their level, reset them to level 1 or put them back to their original level.

Hired pawns can be set to any level, up or down.

'Allow hired pawns to level from EXP' (Options + Debug) lets hired pawns level up as you play, just like your main pawn.

'Apply skills as the upgraded variant' gives skills their upgraded form, like Compass Slash becoming Full Moon Slash.

Skills above the pawn's vocation rank are hidden unless you want them.

Master skills and DLC skills can now be applied and show as (Master) or (DLC)/(DLC Reward).

'Keep augments when changing vocation' transfers a pawn's augments when changing vocation.

'No skill' empties a skill slot, and core skills can be reset or restored to what the pawn came with.

By default you only see skills and augments the pawn has already learned, there is a toggle to show everything.  
  
**Name, Personality + Voice**  
Rename pawns and their moniker (the name some menus show), change their personality, specializations or voices.

Every option shows the pawn's original value next to it.

**Note: Make sure to save and reload after changing voice or personality for settings to apply.**  
  
**Behaviour**  
Set chatter to Normal, Less, Rare or Silent to make them finally shut up!

Make them immune to dragonsplague, cure existing progress, or if you're feeling extra evil, infect them to a chosen stage.  
  
**Knowledge**  
Change any pawn's knowledge of quest guidance, route directions, shortcuts and gimmicks like chests, levers and doors.

Override mode changes what they know without touching your save.

Permanent mode changes the knowledge in the save itself, unlock everything, wipe everything, or pick entries one by one.  
  
**Appearance**  
Export a look to a file and import it onto another pawn. Face, body, hair, makeup, the lot. Works pawn to pawn and Arisen to pawn.

**Note: Make sure to save and reload after importing for all settings to correctly apply.**  
  
**Presets + Import/Export**  
Presets save the options you've changed in the mod (a voice, a helmet, level 25) so you can apply them to any new pawn in one click. Copy settings straight from another pawn in your party.

Export a full character file and pick which sections come across on import.

Transfer the appearance from pawn to pawn, like the look of a hired pawn? You can now replicate it!  
**Note: Make sure to save and reload after importing for all settings to correctly apply.**  
  
**QoL + Testing Options**  
Some debug, QoL options have been added:  
Godmode, cannot die, instant revive, infinite stamina, infinite carry weight (the menu shows Light too), always on lantern,  
damage multipliers, size multipliers, heal, teleport to you, kill.  
  
"Respawn pawn shortly after death" stops a hire from going back to the Rift, they will be revived when their death timer runs out.  
  
"Validate Pawn" runs the game's own upload check on a pawn and tells you if anything would fail, such as gear their vocation can't use, flags any special skills or appraisal rolls a real drop couldn't have, skills above the pawn's vocation rank.  
  
  
────────────────────  
  
  
**Main Pawn**  
Supports the same options as hired pawns including full import/export.  
Be careful when applying options to the main pawn, some changes are permanent.  
Every time you rest, the REFramework log notes your pawn's upload and whether the server accepted it.  
  
**Your Arisen**  
The QoL section works on you too. Export your own look or import one from a file.  
New in 2.1: enhance or Dragonforge your own gear and edit its special skills. Enhancing puts on an enhanced copy and keeps your original in the bag, Restore puts it back on.  
  
  
**Misc**  
An item spawner with a filter which pulls directly from the game.  
"Remove all dropped items" clears everything you've left lying on the ground. Move or teleport away from it first.  
  
  
────────────────────  
  
  
**Install**  
Needs REFramework. Install with Fluffy Mod Manager or drop the reframework folder into your game folder.  
In-game, open the REFramework menu (Insert by default), go to Script Generated UI, then The Pawn Controller.  
A short first start guide opens the first time, and you can reopen it from 'How to use'.  
**Anything shown in yellow has been changed from its default.**
